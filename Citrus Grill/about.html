About Citrus Grill

Our Story

Citrus Grill was born from a passion for authentic, fresh Kenyan cuisine. We believe that great food starts with quality ingredients sourced directly from local farmers who share our commitment to excellence.

Every dish on our menu tells a story of craftsmanship, innovation, and a deep love for the vibrant flavors of Kenya. From our signature citrus-marinated grills to our farm-to-table vegetables, we take pride in delivering an unforgettable dining experience.
