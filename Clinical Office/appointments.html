Appointment FAQs

How long before I get a confirmation?

We typically confirm your appointment within 24 hours via phone or email. If you don't hear from us, please call to confirm.

Can I change my appointment after booking?

Yes! Contact us at least 48 hours before your appointment. We'll do our best to reschedule for your preferred time.

What if I need to cancel?

Please provide at least 24 hours notice. You can call or email us to cancel. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

How much does a consultation cost?

Consultation fees vary by service. Pricing will be provided during confirmation. We accept insurance and multiple payment methods.

Do I need to bring anything?

Bring a valid ID, any insurance documents, and a list of current medications. First-time patients should arrive 10 minutes early.
