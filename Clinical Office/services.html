Our Services

Comprehensive healthcare solutions tailored to your needs

General Consultation

Our primary care service provides thorough medical evaluation and diagnosis for any health concern.

What's Included:

  • Comprehensive health history and physical examination
  • Symptom assessment and diagnosis
  • Treatment recommendations
  • Health counseling and lifestyle advice

Duration:

30-45 minutes per consultation

Best For:

New patients, routine check-ups, acute health concerns

Chronic Disease Management

Long-term care for conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and other chronic illnesses.

What's Included:

  • Regular monitoring and follow-up consultations
  • Medication management and optimization
  • Lifestyle modification support
  • Complication screening and prevention

Conditions Managed:

Diabetes, Hypertension, COPD, Asthma, Heart Disease, Thyroid Disorders

Frequency:

Customized schedule based on your condition

Medication & Prescriptions

Evidence-based medication therapy management and safe prescription practices.

What's Included:

  • Prescription writing and medication counseling
  • Drug interaction checking
  • Side effect monitoring
  • Medication reviews and optimization

Why Choose Our Service:

We only prescribe medications when necessary and always explore non-pharmaceutical options first.

Laboratory Tests

Comprehensive diagnostic testing for accurate disease detection and monitoring.

Tests Available:

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • Metabolic Panel & Liver Function Tests
  • Lipid Profile & Glucose Testing
  • Urinalysis & Thyroid Function Tests
  • HIV/AIDS Screening
  • COVID-19 Testing

Turnaround Time:

Most results available within 24-48 hours

Imaging & Ultrasound

Advanced diagnostic imaging to visualize internal organs and structures.

Services Offered:

  • Abdominal Ultrasound
  • Obstetric Ultrasound (Pregnancy Monitoring)
  • Thyroid Ultrasound
  • Chest X-rays
  • Bone Density Screening

Technology:

State-of-the-art equipment with expert interpretation

Preventive Care & Health Screening

Proactive health programs to prevent disease and maintain wellness.

Screening Programs:

  • Annual Health Check-ups
  • Cancer Screening (Cervical, Breast, Colon)
  • Cardiovascular Risk Assessment
  • Immunization Services
  • Wellness Counseling

Age-Specific Screenings:

Customized screening plans based on age, risk factors, and family history

Vaccinations & Immunization

Full immunization services for children and adults following WHO guidelines.

Vaccines Available:

  • Childhood Vaccinations (Complete Schedule)
  • Adult Boosters & Travel Vaccines
  • Influenza & Pneumococcal Vaccines
  • HPV Vaccination
  • Tetanus & Hepatitis Vaccines

Health Education & Counseling

Guidance and support for lifestyle modifications and disease management.

Topics Covered:

  • Nutrition & Healthy Eating
  • Exercise & Physical Activity
  • Weight Management
  • Stress Management & Mental Health
  • Smoking Cessation
