Getting Here

🚗 By Car

From the city center, head towards Kenyatta Avenue. Turn right on Health Street. Our clinic is on the left side, marked with the blue and green sign. Free parking is available in the compound.

🚕 By Taxi/Uber

Give your driver this address: 123 Health Street, Nairobi, Kenya 00100. The clinic is easily accessible from major taxi stands and Uber is widely available in the area.

🚌 By Public Transport

Several matatu routes pass near our location. Exit at Kenyatta Avenue or Health Street junction. The clinic is within walking distance from the main transport hubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring when visiting?

Please bring a valid ID, any insurance documents, and a list of current medications. First-time patients should allow 10 minutes for registration.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, emergency services are available 24/7. Call +254 700 123 456 and use the emergency hotline for urgent medical situations.

How can I reach you outside working hours?

Outside clinic hours, you can reach our emergency line at +254 700 123 456 for urgent medical assistance.

Is your clinic accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes, our clinic is fully wheelchair accessible with accessible restrooms and parking. If you need special accommodations, please let us know when booking.
