Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and clinic

About Clinical Office

Who is the medical team at Clinical Office?

Clinical Office is led by experienced medical professionals including Dr. James Kipchoge (Chief Medical Officer with 16 years of experience) and Dr. Sarah Ochieng (Diagnostic Specialist with 12 years of experience). Both are registered with the Kenya Medical Practitioners & Dentists Board. Learn more about our team.

How long has Clinical Office been operating?

Clinical Office was founded over 15 years ago with a mission to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare. We've grown into a trusted healthcare destination known for our patient-centered approach.

What certifications does the clinic have?

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified, HCQA accredited, biohazard safety certified, and compliant with GDPR and local data protection laws. All medical practitioners are registered with Kenya Medical Practitioners & Dentists Board.

Is the clinic accredited by insurance providers?

Yes, we work with all major health insurance providers in Kenya including NHIF, Jubilee Insurance, UAP Insurance, Britam Healthcare, and many others. Please verify your specific coverage details.

Appointments & Scheduling

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online using our appointment booking form, or contact us directly at +254 700 123 456 or appointments@clinicaloffice.co.ke. We typically confirm your appointment within 24 hours.

What are your operating hours?

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM
Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed
Emergency Services: Available 24/7

How far in advance should I book?

You can book appointments at least 2-3 days in advance. For urgent concerns, we try to accommodate same-day appointments when possible. Call us directly for expedited scheduling.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule your appointment. Please contact us at least 48 hours before your scheduled time. You can call +254 700 123 456 or email appointments@clinicaloffice.co.ke.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance are free. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) or no-shows may incur a consultation fee. We understand medical situations can be unpredictable—just communicate with us.

Do you have online or telemedicine consultations?

Currently, we offer in-person consultations only. However, follow-up consultations for stable chronic conditions can be discussed. Contact us for specific arrangements.

First-Time Visits

What should I bring for my first visit?

Please bring: (1) Valid ID, (2) Insurance card or NHIF number if applicable, (3) List of current medications, (4) Medical history summary if possible, (5) Any previous medical records or test results. This helps us provide you with the best care from day one.

How early should I arrive for my appointment?

Please arrive 10-15 minutes early for your first visit to complete registration and health history forms. For follow-up appointments, arriving 5 minutes early is sufficient.

How long does a first consultation take?

A first consultation typically takes 45-60 minutes, including registration and health assessment. Follow-up visits are usually 30-45 minutes. We don't rush—your health deserves our full attention.

Do I need a referral to visit the clinic?

No referral is required. Clinical Office operates as a private primary care clinic. You can visit directly for any health concern. If you need specialist care, we'll provide appropriate referrals.

What documents will I receive after my visit?

After your consultation, you'll receive a visit summary, prescription (if needed), and instructions for follow-up care. These are provided for your records and to share with other healthcare providers if needed.

Services & Treatment

What services do you offer?

We offer a full range of primary healthcare services including general consultation, chronic disease management, medication review, laboratory tests, imaging/ultrasound, preventive care, vaccinations, and health counseling. View all services.

Do you conduct laboratory tests on-site?

Yes, we have in-house laboratory facilities for common tests (blood tests, urinalysis, etc.). More specialized tests are sent to our certified partner laboratories. Results are usually available within 24-48 hours.

What is your approach to prescribing medications?

We follow evidence-based medicine principles and only prescribe when necessary. We always discuss non-pharmacological options first, explain your medications, and monitor for side effects. Your informed consent matters to us.

Do you offer preventive health screenings?

Yes, we offer comprehensive preventive care including age-appropriate cancer screenings, cardiovascular risk assessment, bone density screening, and immunizations. Prevention is a key part of our approach.

Can I get prescription refills without visiting?

For stable chronic conditions, you may be able to get prescription refills via phone or email after initial consultation. Contact us to arrange this. This assumes your condition is well-controlled and doesn't require examination.

Do you handle emergency situations?

For life-threatening emergencies, please call 999 or go to the nearest emergency hospital. We provide urgent same-day care for non-emergency urgent conditions. Call us immediately at +254 700 123 456.

Insurance & Payment

What insurance plans do you accept?

We work with NHIF, Jubilee Insurance, UAP Insurance, Britam Healthcare, and many other providers. We recommend calling ahead to verify your specific coverage and any requirements like authorization codes.

How does billing work with insurance?

You can pay directly to us and claim reimbursement from your insurance, or if your insurance allows direct billing, we can submit claims on your behalf. Discuss this during your visit or when booking.

What if I don't have insurance?

No problem! We serve uninsured patients and offer transparent pricing. We can discuss payment plans and flexible payment options based on your financial situation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, M-Pesa, debit cards, credit cards, and insurance claims. Payment is typically due at the end of your visit, and we can arrange payment plans if needed.

How much does a consultation cost?

Consultation fees vary by service type. General consultation fees are reasonable and competitive. We'll provide transparent pricing during appointment confirmation and can discuss insurance coverage.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for expensive procedures or treatments, we can arrange payment plans. Discuss this with our staff—we want healthcare to be accessible to everyone.

Medical Concerns & Privacy

Is my medical information confidential?

Absolutely. All your medical information is strictly confidential and protected by law. We comply with healthcare privacy regulations and GDPR. Your information is never shared without your explicit consent.

What if I'm not comfortable with my current treatment?

Your comfort and trust are paramount. Please tell your doctor if you have concerns. We're happy to discuss alternative options or get a second opinion from our other physicians.

Can I get my medical records?

Yes, you have the right to access your medical records. Submit a written request, and we'll provide copies within 5-7 business days. There may be a small copying fee.

What if I need a specialist?

If your condition requires specialized care, we'll provide referrals to trusted specialists. We maintain good relationships with specialists in Nairobi and can facilitate smooth transitions.

How do you handle chronic disease management?

We take a holistic, patient-centered approach with regular monitoring, medication optimization, lifestyle counseling, and complication screening. We believe in treating the whole person, not just symptoms.

Accessibility & Facilities

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the entire clinic is wheelchair accessible including entrances, examination rooms, and restrooms. If you need special accommodations, please let us know when booking.

Is there parking available?

Yes, free parking is available in our compound for patients and visitors. Reserved spaces are available for people with disabilities.

What's the waiting area like?

Our waiting area is clean, comfortable, and climate-controlled. We provide refreshments and WiFi for our patients. We also make efforts to minimize wait times through efficient scheduling.

Do you have facilities for children?

Yes, our clinic is child-friendly with a comfortable pediatric consultation area. We welcome parents/guardians and ensure children receive age-appropriate care and communication.
